Project Name: CrewSync

Student Name: Ekamjeet Singh

Problem Statement:
College event organisers struggle to coordinate volunteer duties through group chats and spreadsheets. This leads to understaffed shifts, overlapping commitments, unclear replacement responsibility, and inconsistent attendance records.

Solution:
CrewSync provides an organiser and volunteer workspace for publishing events, joining conflict-checked shifts, transferring assignments through replacement requests, and recording attendance.

Tech Stack:

Major Features:
1. Organiser-owned event and shift creation.
2. Capacity-aware volunteer joining with scheduling conflict prevention.
3. Atomic replacement acceptance with responsibility retained until transfer.
4. Owner-authorised attendance after a shift starts.
5. Coverage dashboard, event search/filtering, activity history, and cancellation.
